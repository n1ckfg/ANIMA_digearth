Recycling the Dead
Bodies are made of other bodies, people grow from others death. Through procreation, through ingestion, through the dissemination of knowledge and the ability to create we build new people from those that already exist and all of those that have existed in the past.

Recycling the Dead features four portraits. Each portrait emphasizes a different aspect of the recycling process. Just as we cannot separate our bodies and our minds we cannot make any clear distinctions between different people, or even between people and the objects integral to their person.

As we explore the bodies of the subjects with our mouse and similar tools we uncover more about each character and the process that they are undergoing. A narrative is gradually uncovered as we touch and delve into various parts of their (extended) anatomy. Who is it that decides what people to create and how to alter the ones that exist? Who is in control of the recycling process, and who directs the paths of our various components?

Decomposition can be a constructive process... as people get recycled.

Lena Olin
March 1997
